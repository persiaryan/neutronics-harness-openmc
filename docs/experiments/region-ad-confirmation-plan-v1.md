# Proposed confirmation after the A/D development pilot

Status: **design proposal; twelve unqualified candidate specifications created
on 2026-10-18**. See the [candidate suite and qualification plan](region-confirmation-tasks-v1.md).
No qualified held-out reference set, justified sample size or confirmation run
has been created. The completed experiment remains the frozen 36-slot development
pilot. The candidate suite does not modify that pilot or its historical records.

## Decision after the pilot

First distinguish technical feasibility from scientific benefit:

- Can both A/D authoring and independent final assessment finish under the fixed
  budgets, with attributable incidents and complete evidence?
- Is the annotation procedure usable on final sources, including uncertain cases?
- Are differences concentrated in the targeted mechanism, other geometry,
  settings, model configuration, or consumed resources?
- How much uncertainty remains because only three development tasks and three
  repeats per cell were used?

If the pilot pauses on an infrastructure/evaluator incident, preserve all consumed
slots and diagnose that incident before deciding on continuation. Do not replace
failed slots or label a partially observed comparison as completed. If the
evaluator changes, version the protocol and retain original results rather than
silently rescoring them.

A reduction of the targeted defect with unchanged global success motivates a
local mechanism claim, with appropriate uncertainty. A global improvement on
reused tasks motivates confirmation; it does not itself establish generalization.
A null or mixed pilot result is also reportable, especially if it identifies
limits of procedural assistance or failures outside the targeted mechanism.

## Candidate task coverage for a future proposal

Task families to consider, without fixing new physical assumptions here:

- Reused intersections with complements and cell references.
- Reused unions and mixed Boolean expressions.
- Nested shared children, where a new outer expression does not remove aliasing.
- Correct intentional in-place construction on unshared regions.
- Models whose dominant difficulties concern other geometry or physical settings.

The eventual selection must contain more than superficial parameter copies if
the claim concerns broader modeling behavior. New public specifications and
independently authored private references need separate review and qualification.
Task requirements and finite observer coverage must be explicit.

A task becomes a held-out confirmation task only after its assignment and
reference manifest are frozen and no development/tuning has used its outcomes.
The families above are proposals, not a currently reserved benchmark.

## Sample size and analysis to freeze before calls

Use the pilot only to inform feasibility, rough rates, incidence of unscored
outcomes and variability. It cannot reliably estimate between-task generalization
from three tasks.

Before confirmation, specify:

1. The smallest practically relevant improvement in complete protocol success.
2. Whether the target is precision of an estimated difference or a powered test,
   and how repeated generations within a task and model are handled.
3. The number and selection of new task families, repeats and model setups.
4. Treatment of incidents and missing outcomes, retaining the all-assigned
   denominator and explicit conditional analyses.
5. The primary contrast and secondary mechanism analyses, including any multiple
   comparisons and subgroup reporting.
6. Blinded mechanism review where feasible, a reviewer/adjudication procedure,
   and explicit uncertain/unassessable labels.
7. Frozen skill, prompts, provider setup identities, images, data, references,
   evaluator version, budgets, order randomization and stopping rules.

Do not choose a favorable sample size or success criterion after looking at
confirmation results. Do not estimate a causal effect by comparing skill loaders
with non-loaders: loading is a behavior after assignment.

For the original two-model A/D design, a future inventory of T tasks and R repeats
would contain 4 × T × R assignments and at most 64 × T × R model requests under
the 16-request profile, plus separately budgeted qualification and independent
final assessment. This is accounting, not a sample-size recommendation.

## Relation to native skill selection

D supplies the entire procedure initially. If the paper also claims agent-selected
skill use, that requires the separate E hypothesis and condition, with catalog
exposure, actual body delivery, application evidence and independent final
assessment distinguished.

Adding E changes the design and budget; it must be declared before confirmation.
The A/D pilot cannot validate E's spontaneous selection. Neither procedural
assistance nor matching a computational reference alone qualifies nuclear safety
or universal physical correctness.

## Completed pilot input — 2026-10-17

The [completed pilot](region-ad-pilot-result-v1.md) consumed 36 assignments and
147 pilot requests, with 30 complete successes, six evaluated failures and no
infrastructure incidents. The descriptive D-minus-A difference is +11.1 percentage
points within each model setup. This estimate rests on only nine assignments
per arm/model and three reused tasks; it is not a dependable power-planning
effect size.

Before selecting a confirmation budget, adjudicate the mechanism review and
scientific reference/threshold choices. The observed Luna reflective-cell
regression and non-targeted defects justify retaining broad full-protocol
success and task-level reporting. Do not tune the fixed procedure on future
confirmation outcomes. This checkpoint does not create new held-out tasks,
approve a sample size, launch E, or authorize a confirmation campaign.
